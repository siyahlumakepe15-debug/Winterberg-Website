Academic & Practical Programs

Winterberg Agricultural High School combines standard academic subjects with comprehensive hands-on farming, preparing students for careers in agriculture and skilled trades.

Curriculum

  • Core academic subjects (Grades 8–12)
  • Agricultural sciences
  • Livestock management
  • Welding and metalwork
  • Meat processing
  • Mohair classing and sheep shearing

Duty Roster

Weekly agricultural practicals timetable by grade:

Grade Practical Focus Day
Grade 8 Poultry & vegetable gardens Monday
Grade 9 Piggery Tuesday
Grade 10 Dairy herd Wednesday
Grade 11 Citrus orchards & lucerne Thursday
Grade 12 Welding, metalwork & meat processing Friday

Safety Protocols

Mandatory safety manuals cover the correct handling of machinery and chemicals used on the farm. All students must complete safety induction before taking part in practicals.

  • Machinery operating procedures
  • Chemical handling and storage guidelines
  • Protective equipment requirements
  • Emergency procedures
