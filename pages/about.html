About Us

Winterberg Agricultural High School started in 1972 as an all-boys school in the Kat River Valley near Fort Beaufort, South Africa. The school provides academics and farming as a purpose, merging formal academic education with practical and commercial farming experience.

Our History

Since 1972, Winterberg Agricultural High School has grown from a small all-boys institution into a respected centre for agricultural education in the Eastern Cape, guided by the motto Ut Colant Et Custodiant – To cultivate and care for.

Our Farm

Our working farm enterprise features greenhouses, tractor sheds and fields, alongside a dairy herd, piggery, poultry units, citrus orchards and lucerne production.

  • Dairy herd
  • Piggery
  • Poultry units
  • Citrus orchards
  • Lucerne
  • Greenhouses and tractor sheds

Sustainability Policy

Winterberg Agricultural High School is committed to eco-friendly farming practices that protect the Kat River Valley for future generations while giving students hands-on experience in sustainable agriculture.

Vision and Mission

  • Combined curriculum integrating standard academic subjects with hands-on farming
  • Practical facilities including a dairy herd, piggery, poultry units, citrus orchards and lucerne
  • Training in specialised rural skills such as welding, metalwork and meat processing

Who We Serve

  • High school students in grades 8 through 12 seeking vocational training alongside academics
  • Future agricultural professionals aiming for careers in livestock management, dairy production and citrus farming
  • Students learning trade skills such as welding, metalwork, mohair classing and sheep shearing
  • Local shops and community members trading with the school farm
